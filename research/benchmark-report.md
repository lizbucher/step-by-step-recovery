# Benchmark Report — Step by Step Treatment and Recovery Centers

5 sober-living / recovery-housing reference sites benchmarked. Sources: WebFetch live captures, May 2026.

## 1. Harris House (St. Louis — direct local competitor)

- **Headline:** *"Trusted Addiction Treatment in St. Louis"*
- **Sub:** *"For more than 60 years, Harris House has provided compassionate, evidence-based addiction treatment in St. Louis, Missouri."*
- **CTAs:** Get Help Today / Check your insurance
- **Trust signals:** 60+ years, CARF, MO DMH, BBB A+, United Way
- **Palette / type:** Professional blue/teal + warm accent; clean sans
- **Distinctive move:** *"Treat the Whole Person — Body, Mind & Spirit"* philosophy threaded throughout
- **Voice:** Compassionate, professional nonprofit
- **What's missing for premium audience:** No outcomes data, no provider credentials, no facility imagery that signals "premium residence" (institutional architecture), zero pricing transparency, no founder story.

## 2. Rize OC (premium California rehab landing page)

- **Headline:** *"Medically-Supervised Drug & Alcohol Detox Center"*
- **Sub:** *"Safe and private detox program covered by your insurance."*
- **CTAs:** Call Now (phone) / Start Online Intake Form
- **Trust signals:** 1000+ Successful Recoveries, 139+ 5-Star Reviews, Joint Commission, LegitScript, DHCS, ASAM, "PhD Clinical Leadership," 20+ Yrs Experience
- **Palette / type:** Coastal neutrals + calming blue, sans
- **Photography:** Coastal California, serene, aspirational
- **Distinctive move:** Frames detox as a *coastal sanctuary*, not a clinic — luxury-resort language with medical authority underneath
- **Voice:** *Compassionate sophistication meets clinical authority*
- **Premium signals:** White-glove service language, max 12 clients, gourmet meals, executive workspaces, equine therapy.

## 3. Serenity Lodge Lake Arrowhead (luxury mountain rehab)

- **Headline:** *"Drug & Alcohol Addiction Treatment"*
- **Sub:** *"Compassionate, evidence-based care for individuals struggling with drug addiction. Our secluded mountain setting supports healing from the inside out."*
- **CTAs:** Call (phone) / Start Your Recovery
- **Trust signals:** 4.7★ from 815+ clients, 94% satisfaction, 87% completion (above national avg), 40+ insurance carrier logos, Joint Commission, ASAM, CARF, SAMHSA
- **Palette / type:** Calm blue/green + warm earth, sans
- **Distinctive move:** Hard-frequency phone CTA — every section repeats it
- **Voice:** Supportive yet authoritative
- **Luxury signals:** 22-acre campus, Olympic pool, recording studio, meditation pyramid, sauna, gym, movie theatre, private lodging.

## 4. The Althea Project (community nonprofit sober living)

- **Headline:** *"Sober Living"* (literally repeated three times — no sub-head)
- **CTAs:** Donate Now / Pay Now (rent)
- **Trust signals:** Founded 2023, downloadable handbook, mission language, business hours
- **Palette / type:** Teal logo + white bg + neutrals, sans
- **Photography:** Stock wellness/gardening imagery
- **Distinctive move:** Almost nothing — page is functional, not designed
- **Emotional appeal:** *Belonging and hope* — "feels like home" framing
- **Why benchmark:** This is the *floor* — the design ceiling Step by Step must clear by an order of magnitude.

## 5. Caron (premium private-pay treatment campus — gold-standard reference)

- **Headline:** *"Transforming Lives Through Addiction Treatment"*
- **Sub:** *"Caron's expert staff has helped thousands of people and their families transform their lives, combining neurorestorative care with proven treatment modalities."*
- **CTAs:** Start your journey today / Learn more
- **Trust signals:** 70 Years of Results (1957), 115k patients, 90% post-treatment satisfaction, CARF, NAATP, Newsweek
- **Palette / type:** Neutral institutional whites/grays/nature, sans
- **Photography:** Lifestyle (couples, nature, therapeutic settings) — *not* clinical
- **Distinctive move:** Stat-anchored hero ("70 Years / 115k Patients / 90%") — credentials pitched as outcomes
- **Voice:** Compassionate authority
- **Premium markers:** Specialty programs (LGBTQ+, healthcare professionals, executives, "affluent adults"), branded program tiers (Grand View, Ocean Drive), alumni community emphasis.

## Top 6 patterns to steal

1. **Stat-anchored trust strip directly under the hero** (source: Caron) — three single-number facts, max. For Step by Step: years of recovery experience (Yomi) / single house intimacy (8 residents) / target accreditation tier.
2. **Named approach / philosophy section** (source: Harris "Whole Person," Caron "neurorestorative") — Step by Step gets *"The Step by Step Method"* — five named pillars.
3. **Lifestyle photography over clinical** (source: Caron, Rize) — humans living, not patients being treated.
4. **Real facility tour / virtual gallery** (source: Harris virtual tour, Serenity campus) — for Step by Step this is the wedge: walk-the-house gallery.
5. **Alumni / outcomes voice** (source: Caron, Serenity) — first-name testimonials embedded mid-page, not siloed on a Reviews tab.
6. **Phone in the sticky header** (source: Serenity, Harris, Rize) — recovery decisions are time-sensitive; phone must be 1 tap from anywhere.

---

## What to AVOID copying

- **Insurance-verification widget as the primary conversion goal** (Rize, Serenity). Step by Step is positioned private-pay/investor-grade; an insurance widget would muddy the premium read. Use a gentle "Inquire about availability" intake instead, with insurance discussion as a downstream conversation.
- **22-acre amenity-stack messaging** (Serenity). Step by Step is a single modern home; the differentiator is *intimacy and design*, not amenity volume.
- **Donate-led conversion** (Althea). Step by Step is a for-profit residence and an investment opportunity, not a charity.
- **Stock-imagery wellness shots** (Althea, parts of Harris). Real photography of the home is the brand.
- **"Treat addiction" framing in the H1.** Lead with the *destination* (recovery, structure, life rebuilt) — see Caron's "Transforming Lives."

## Step by Step's differentiation thesis

> *None of the five references lead with the actual residence as the hero. Harris House shows institutional buildings; Caron shows landscapes; Rize sells coastline; Serenity sells campus. Step by Step's modern home in a St. Louis residential neighborhood is the unique visual asset — and the entire visual identity should be built around making that home the protagonist of the brand.*

Five-pillar wedge:

1. **The home is the brand.** Architectural photography of the residence is the hero, period.
2. **Boutique scale, by design.** 8 residents, not 22 acres. Intimacy is the premium signal — every detail is considered, like a private members' residence.
3. **St. Louis private-pay whitespace.** Harris House owns nonprofit/insurance. There is no premium private-pay sober living in St. Louis with this aesthetic. The market gap is real.
4. **Founder-led, not staff-led.** Yomi Martin's story is the trust anchor. Caron talks about "our expert staff." Step by Step talks about Yomi.
5. **Dual-audience site (the investor wedge).** A discrete *Partner With Us* page lets the same site function as Yomi's investor-pitch credibility piece — invisible to the casual visitor, prominent to the intended one.
